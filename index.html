About KCC

A legacy of faith and education since 1927

Building knowledge, character, and competence

Kabankalan Catholic College has served the community of Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental since 1927. KCC forms learners through academic learning, Catholic faith formation, community service, and holistic development.

Our mission: To provide quality and inclusive Catholic education, rooted in the Gospel and driven by innovative research and meaningful community engagements.
